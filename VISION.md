# TradingView Replica: Project Vision

## Our Mission

To create a professional-grade trading platform that serves as the foundation for our trader funding program, enabling aspiring traders to prove their skills, earn capital allocation, and build sustainable trading careers.

## Vision Statement

We envision a complete ecosystem where talented traders can be discovered, evaluated, and funded without the traditional barriers to entry in professional trading. Our TradingView-inspired platform will be the centerpiece of this ecosystem, providing both the analytical tools traders need and the performance tracking capabilities our business requires to identify profitable traders worthy of capital allocation.

## Business Context

Our primary business is a **trader funding program** similar to Topstep, where:

1. Traders join evaluation challenges to prove their trading abilities
2. Successful traders receive allocation of our capital to trade
3. Profits are shared between the trader and our company
4. Performance is continuously monitored against risk parameters

The trading platform is not just a tool but the core interface through which our entire business operates, serving both trader needs and our business requirements.

## Core Principles

### 1. Performance First
- Charts must render smoothly, even with large datasets
- Trade execution must be responsive with minimal latency
- Resource usage should be optimized for all devices
- Real-time data updates should be efficient and reliable

### 2. Accuracy and Reliability
- Technical indicators must be mathematically correct
- Price data must be displayed with high fidelity
- Execution and tracking systems must be failsafe
- System status should be transparent to users

### 3. Progressive Complexity
- Basic features should be immediately usable without training
- Advanced features should be discoverable as traders progress
- Expert tools should be available but not overwhelming
- Educational content should guide users to better trading practices

### 4. Trader Evaluation
- Clear metrics for evaluating trader performance
- Transparent rule enforcement within the platform
- Automated tracking of key performance indicators
- Unbiased assessment of trading skill

### 5. Business Integration
- Platform must support our funding program business model
- Seamless progression from evaluation to funded accounts
- Robust risk management and compliance features
- Analytics for business decision-making

## User Personas

### Alex: The Evaluation Trader
- Recently joined our evaluation program
- Looking to prove trading skill and earn funded account
- Needs clear understanding of evaluation rules and metrics
- Values transparency in performance tracking

### Taylor: The Funded Trader
- Has passed evaluation and trades with allocated capital
- Requires advanced charting and execution capabilities
- Relies on drawing tools and indicators for strategy
- Needs real-time performance metrics against rules

### Morgan: The Professional
- Experienced trader managing significant allocated capital
- Requires precise indicator calculations for complex strategies
- Creates sophisticated multi-timeframe analysis
- Values stability and reliability above all

### Jordan: The Business Analyst
- Internal team member monitoring trader performance
- Requires comprehensive data on trader activity
- Uses platform to identify successful trading patterns
- Values reporting tools and risk management features

## Feature Roadmap

### Phase 1: Core Trading Platform (Current)
- Interactive price charts with multiple timeframes
- Basic technical indicators (MA, RSI, MACD)
- Drawing tools for analysis
- Symbol search and selection
- Responsive design and performance metrics

### Phase 2: Evaluation Program Infrastructure
- Trader account creation and management
- Rule-based performance tracking
- Automated enforcement of trading parameters
- Risk management dashboards
- Evaluation progress tracking

### Phase 3: Funded Account Management
- Capital allocation system
- Profit splitting calculations
- Advanced performance analytics
- Trading plan documentation tools
- Risk parameter customization

### Phase 4: Ecosystem Expansion
- Trading community and communication tools
- Trader education and certification programs
- Analytics for trader selection and development
- API access for advanced integrations
- Trading algorithm evaluation and funding

## Success Metrics

1. **Business Growth**: Increasing number of evaluation and funded traders
2. **Platform Performance**: Charts render in under 500ms and maintain 60fps during interaction
3. **Trader Success Rate**: Improved percentage of traders passing evaluation
4. **Capital Efficiency**: Higher returns on allocated capital
5. **Retention**: Funded traders remain active in the program long-term

## Non-Goals

- We are **not** trying to become a general-purpose social trading platform
- We are **not** aiming to compete with brokerages on execution-only services
- We are **not** focused on supporting assets outside our funding program scope
- We are **not** attempting to provide general market analysis services

## Development Philosophy

Our development philosophy embraces:

1. **Business-Driven Development**: Prioritize features that drive our funding business model
2. **Trader-Centered Design**: Base decisions on trader needs within our program context
3. **Technical Excellence**: Build a robust platform that can scale with our business
4. **Continuous Evaluation**: Regularly assess if technical features support business goals
5. **Data-Informed Decisions**: Use platform data to improve both trader experience and business outcomes

This vision document serves as our guiding light for building a trading platform that powers our trader funding business. When making decisions, we should ask: "Does this help us identify, evaluate, and support profitable traders?" If not, we should reconsider or adjust our approach.

Remember that our ultimate goal is not just to build a charting platform, but to create the technical foundation for a successful trader funding program that benefits both talented traders and our business.
